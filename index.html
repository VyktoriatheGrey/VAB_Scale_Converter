VAB Scale Converter

Vyktoria's Absolute Breast Scale

Welcome! If you have ever wanted to find out what size your female or futa OC's breasts are, then the VABS converter is for you! If you have a reference sheet that is "accurate" in proportions and is easily measurable, then you can input that data into the VABS converter and find out how big your boobs are on Vyktoria's Absolute Breast Scale! It will give you the diameter of your OC's boob, its projection (how far it sticks out from the ribcage), volume (milk!), and weight! The scale attempts to convert real world scaling used for bra sizes and extrapolates the scale out to "reasonable" scales (capping out at around 148m/484ft)—still pretty large! Anything beyond that felt like using other scales (city, continent, planetary sized, etc) felt more appropriate.

How to Use

  1. Select a Unit of Measurement mode:
    • Pixels / Metric — real height in cm, drawing measurements in pixels
    • Pixels / Imperial (ft-in) — real height in feet+inches, drawing measurements in pixels
    • Imperial (ft-in) — real height in feet+inches, drawing measurements in inches
    • Metric — real height in cm, drawing measurements in cm
  2. Enter the character’s in-universe full height (always real-world units).
  3. Measure your OC on your reference sheet and input the length into the "Measured Length of OC" field.
  4. Enter the measured diameter of ONE breast from your ref sheet (widest point) into the "Measured Diameter of OC's Breast" field.
  5. Scale factor, real diameter, VAB Index, projection, volume and weight will be calculated for you!

About the VAB Scale

This is an absolute geometric scale for breast size. The letter encodes the absolute size of the breasts (diameter, projection, volume, weight) relative to a fixed real-world baseline, independent of the character's underbust or overall body size.

Baseline (Letter A / Index 0)

  • Reference model: 6'0" (183 cm) woman, 34" underbust, real-world A-cup equivalent
  • Diameter (one breast): 10.6 cm (4.17 in)
  • Starting shape: near-hemispherical (sphericity 0.50)

Growth Rule

  • Diameter increases by a constant +0.85 cm per Index step (average real-world step from A to G)
  • This linear diameter growth is continued uniformly across all 18,278 sizes

Letter Tiers

  • Single letters A–Z: 26 sizes (Index 0–25)
  • Double letters AA–ZZ: 676 sizes (Index 26–701) — all letter combinations
  • Triple letters AAA–ZZZ: 17,576 sizes (Index 702–18,277) — all letter combinations
  • Total: 18,278 sizes

Geometry / Sphericity

  • Index 0–8 (A–I): Sphericity rises linearly from 0.50 (hemisphere) toward 0.95
  • Index 9+ (J and above): Sphericity locked at 0.95 (near-spherical)
  • Projection = Sphericity × Diameter
  • 0.95 was chosen so the breasts read as strongly spherical to the eye while avoiding the uncanny “perfect floating ball” look of a true 1.00 sphere.

Volume Formula

  • r = Diameter / 2
  • Hemisphere volume = (2/3) × π × r³
  • Volume (one breast) = Hemisphere volume × (2 × Sphericity)
  • At s=0.50 → pure hemisphere; at s=0.95 → 95% of the way to a full sphere

Weight

  • Density = 0.90 g/cm³

Notes

  • Underbust / body-type variation is secondary and does not change the VAB letter.
  • All values are idealized geometric estimates for consistent fictional scaling.
  • Use the Ref Sheet Converter tab to turn drawing measurements into a VAB Index / letter.
